AAOIFI Sharia Compliance

We actively filter out companies involved in unethical industries or those burdened by heavy interest-based debt. This ensures our capital grows on pure, real-world productivity rather than financial speculation.

Quality Over Quantity — Filter for Strong Businesses:

Due to strict ethical filters, we focus on approximately 1/3rd of the total stocks listed in the market. Rather than a restriction, this acts as a powerful quality filter—automatically concentrating our capital in asset-rich, fundamentally sound companies with low debt profiles. We completely avoid highly volatile Futures & Options (F&O) and rumors-based speculative trading, ensuring our trades are backed by actual, productive business operations.

  • Complete Exclusions of Unethical Sectors:
    • Conventional Banking & NBFCs (e.g., HDFC Bank, SBI, Bajaj Finance)
    • Liquor, Pubs, Breweries, and Distilleries (e.g., United Breweries)
    • Gambling, Casinos, Hotels with gaming, and Tobacco (e.g., ITC)
  • Rigorous Financial Ratios:
    • Interest-based income must be under 5% of total revenue
    • Interest-bearing debt must be under 33% of total assets
    • Cash and liquid deposits must be under 33% of total assets
📈

Smart Trend-Following

Instead of guessing when a stock will rise or fall, our system uses simple mathematical checks to detect when a stock is entering a strong, steady upward trend. We only buy stocks that are already showing clear signs of strength and demand.

By removing emotional decisions, the system only enters trades that have statistical probability in their favor. If the market shifts or the momentum fades, the capital protection rules trigger automatically to keep our cash safe.

How the scanning logic works in plain language:

  • Trend Confirmation: We only look at stocks whose short-term average price is trading comfortably above their long-term average price, confirming the buyers are in control.
  • Strength Scoring: A specialized scoring model measures how fast the price is rising compared to normal, ranking stocks on a scale of 0 to 100. We only buy top-ranked setups (Score > 80).
  • Automatic Planning: The system automatically calculates a safe entry point, a protection line (Stop-Loss) to exit if the trade goes wrong, and a profit goal (Target) where we happily cash out.

Regimes & Capital Protection

How HSTS dynamically adjusts exposure sizes to manage market fluctuations and protect capital.

State 1

Macro Bullish

Max Allocation: 20% per trade

The general market index is in a strong upward trend. We deploy maximum permitted capital across our top momentum selections.

State 2

Bear Relief Rally

Max Allocation: 10% per trade

The market shows a short-term bounce but remains weak overall. We trade with half our normal size to keep risk low.

State 3

Capitulation Bottom

Max Allocation: 10% per trade

Markets are extremely panic-sold and bottoming out. We look for high-value reversal trades with tight risk limits.

State 4

Risk-Off (Cash)

Max Allocation: 0% (CASH)

The general market is falling and unsafe. The system locks all trading and keeps 100% of our money safe in cash.

🛡️

Server-Side Protection Orders

Whenever a trade is filled, the system immediately registers a linked protection order (GTT OCO) directly on the exchange servers. If the stock hits our profit goal, it exits; if it hits our safety floor, it drops the trade. This eliminates human hesitation and greed.

📊

Dynamic Cap Enforcement

We restrict each stock position to fit within the regime's allocation cap. High-priced stocks are automatically skipped or limited to 1 share to prevent a single trade from consuming too much capital.
