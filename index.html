Regimes & Capital Protection

How HSTS dynamically adjusts exposure sizes to manage market fluctuations and protect capital.

State 1

Macro Bullish

Max Allocation: 20% per trade

The general market index is in a strong upward trend. We deploy maximum permitted capital across our top momentum selections.

State 2

Bear Relief Rally

Max Allocation: 10% per trade

The market shows a short-term bounce but remains weak overall. We trade with half our normal size to keep risk low.

State 3

Capitulation Bottom

The general market is falling and unsafe. The system locks all trading and keeps 100% of our money safe in cash.

🛡️

Server-Side Protection Orders

Whenever a trade is filled, the system immediately registers a linked protection order (GTT OCO) directly on the exchange servers. If the stock hits our profit goal, it exits; if it hits our safety floor, it drops the trade. This eliminates human hesitation and greed.

📊

Dynamic Cap Enforcement

We restrict each stock position to fit within the regime's allocation cap. High-priced stocks are automatically skipped or limited to 1 share to prevent a single trade from consuming too much capital.
